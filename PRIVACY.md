# Floating Browser Privacy

Effective: July 30, 2026

Floating Browser does not include developer analytics, advertising, or a developer-operated data collection service.

## Data Stored on This Mac

Floating Browser stores the following locally:

- Saved site names and addresses.
- The last visited page address, without its query string, fragment, username, or password.
- Preferences for ad blocking, always-on-top behavior, and window position.
- Website cookies, caches, and local storage managed by WebKit.

Floating Browser does not read or store passwords. The **Open Passwords...** command only launches Apple's Passwords app; copying a credential remains a user-controlled system action.

Use **More > Clear Website Data** to remove WebKit website data. Saved site shortcuts and app preferences are not removed by that command.

## Websites

Pages opened in Floating Browser connect directly to their respective websites. Those websites may collect data under their own privacy policies. Floating Browser's built-in content blocker runs locally and is automatically paused on supported streaming sites for playback compatibility.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the **Issues** tab in the project's GitHub repository. Do not include account credentials, cookies, private URLs, or personal browsing information in a public issue.
